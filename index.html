About

ComicShift is an Android application designed to bridge the gap between PDF and CBZ comic formats. Convert your manga collection easily without uploading files anywhere.

Everything is processed locally on your device, keeping your files private.

Features

📄
PDF → CBZ
📦
CBZ → PDF
🔒
Offline Processing
🆓
Open Source

Why ComicShift?

Many manga readers work best with CBZ files, while many existing collections are stored as PDFs. ComicShift makes it easier to move your manga between different formats and readers.
